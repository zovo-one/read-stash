Read Stash

Version 1.0.0

Save articles, read later. Tags, search, reader mode. Private — your stash never leaves your device. A privacy-first Pocket alternative.

How it works

Click the toolbar icon on any article page to save it to your local stash. Right-click anywhere on a page or on a link for quick-save. The reader mode extracts clean text from saved pages using a built-in heuristic (no third-party library, no cloud). Your library lives entirely in chrome.storage.local — nothing is sent to any server on the Free tier.

Privacy
